Privacy Policy – Duotone

App: Duotone
Package name: com.duotone
Developer: Loods 4.C.a.7.5

This Privacy Policy describes how Duotone handles user data. This page is the official privacy policy for the Duotone application and should be linked from the app store listing.

1. Data collection and use

Duotone displays ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). This service collects the Android Advertising ID when available, app set ID and, where applicable, other device or account identifiers, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These data are used to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted by your settings, analyze performance, and prevent fraud.

The app does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number. Image editing takes place on your device; the app does not upload your images to the developer or the advertising service.

2. Sharing with third parties

The advertising data described above are transmitted to Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google processes and may share these data with its advertising partners under its own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information, and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for details.

3. Data storage & retention

Images and temporary files created for image editing remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. You can remove local app data through Android settings; images you save outside the app may need to be deleted separately. Advertising data sent to Google are retained according to Google’s policies. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete data already held by Google.

4. Security

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit using TLS. Local app files are protected by Android’s storage controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Children

The app is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer using the address below.

6. Advertising choices and data requests

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID using the advertising privacy controls in Android settings, where supported, and manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. Available controls vary by Android version and device. These choices do not necessarily stop ads or collection of other advertising data. For questions or deletion requests, contact the developer; data held by Google are subject to Google’s privacy controls and request procedures.
